How Kitaab Bank Works

A simple, four-step process to give educational books a second life in our school.

1

Student Donates

Enter details of an unused textbook you no longer need and submit it for teacher review.

2

Teacher Verifies

School teachers check the condition and syllabus relevance before publishing to the catalogue.

3

Student Requests

Browse available books for Class 6, 7, or 8 and request what you need without complex registration.

4

School Handover

Collect your verified book directly from the school teacher's desk and start learning.
